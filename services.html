Section 12 • Digital Health & Hospital Information Systems

9 Core Digital Systems Powering Zohran Health Group

HIPAA & PIPEDA Compliant Architecture
System 01

EMR / EHR Platform

Structured clinical documentation, patient history, allergies, problem lists, medication orders, referrals, and continuity of care across all visits.

System 02

Appointment & Patient Registration

Online booking, walk-in registration, digital intake forms, automated SMS/email reminders, and queue management.

System 03

Billing, Insurance & Accounting

Invoicing, insurance claims processing, electronic receipts, financial reporting, and revenue cycle management.

System 04

Laboratory Information System (LIS)

Sample barcode tracking, test ordering, result verification, and physician/patient result reporting as laboratory services expand.

System 05

RIS & PACS Imaging

Radiology Information System (RIS) and Picture Archiving and Communication System (PACS) for imaging workflow, storage, and teleradiology.

System 06

Inventory & Procurement

Tracking medical consumables, diagnostic reagents, clinical equipment maintenance schedules, and approved vendor contracts.

System 07

HR, Credentialing & Payroll

Staff scheduling, medical license and credential verification tracking, mandatory compliance training, and payroll administration.

System 08

Secure Communications & Telehealth

Encrypted patient communication, follow-up reminders, and compliant virtual consultations where permitted by state/provincial law.

System 09

Cybersecurity, Backup & Disaster Recovery

Role-based access control, multi-factor authentication, encryption in transit and at rest, audit logging, and regular backup testing.
