Section 14 & Section 19

7 Diversified Revenue Streams

Zohra Health Group's business model combines recurring primary care, specialist consultations, rehabilitation, and preventive programs:

  1. Primary care and specialist outpatient consultations
  2. Physiotherapy and rehabilitation treatment sessions
  3. Dermatology, diabetes, and nutrition clinic programs
  4. Preventive health check-up and wellness packages
  5. Diagnostic sample collection and imaging coordination
  6. Corporate health and occupational wellness services
  7. Future day-care procedures, inpatient, and surgical services
  • 13 Investment Cost Categories (Section 15)
