Why Patients Choose Zohran Health Group

Complete Multi-Specialty Care Under One Roof

Every Zohran Health Group medical centre brings together primary care doctors, specialists, and diagnostics so your care is seamless:

  1. Same-week family medicine & internal medicine appointments
  2. Direct access to 38 medical, surgical & diagnostic specialties
  3. Cardiology, neurology, orthopaedics, pediatrics & women's health
  4. On-site physiotherapy, dermatology, diabetes & nutrition clinics
  5. Integrated laboratory blood testing, ECG & medical imaging
  6. Annual preventive health screenings & executive wellness plans
  7. Unified digital health record across all US & Canadian locations
  • Board-Certified Physicians & Surgeons

    Every clinician is rigorously credentialed and licensed within their respective US state or Canadian province.

  • Dedicated Care Navigators

    Our patient concierge team coordinates referrals, diagnostic imaging, and insurance verification on your behalf.

  • HIPAA & PIPEDA Protected Privacy

    Your personal health information is encrypted and safeguarded under strict US and Canadian medical privacy laws.
