Section 10 & Section 13 • Activation Prerequisites

Mandatory Readiness Gates Before Specialty Expansion

No clinical specialty is launched until all regulatory, staffing, facility, and safety requirements are verified by the Clinical Quality & Patient Safety Committee.

  • State / Provincial Facility Licensing & Accreditation Full compliance with US state health department or Canadian provincial college facility regulations prior to opening any procedural or inpatient unit.
  • Primary Source Physician & Nurse Credentialing Verified board certification, active medical licensure, malpractice coverage, and clinical privileges approved by the Director of Medical Services.
  • Dedicated Equipment, Sterilization & Infection Control Biomedical calibration, sterilization workflows, medical waste protocols, and radiation safety shielding (for X-ray/CT/Nuclear Medicine).
  • EMR, LIS & RIS/PACS Digital Integration Specialty-specific clinical templates, order sets, laboratory interfaces, and diagnostic imaging archiving active before patient intake.
