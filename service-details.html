Stage A Clinical Scope, Digital Systems & Patient Safety

Sections 9, 12 & 13 of the Zohran Health Group Proposal: How initial outpatient services operate with integrated EMR/EHR technology, clinical governance, and patient safety systems.

Sections 9 & 13 • Clinical Governance

High-Demand Outpatient Care Built on Clinical Governance

Designed to deliver immediate community health value while maintaining hospital-grade quality, credentialing, and safety standards.

The initial medical centre focuses on high-demand, lower-risk outpatient services—Family Medicine, Internal Medicine, Selected Specialist Consultations, Physiotherapy, Dermatology, Diabetes & Endocrinology, Dietary & Nutrition Services, Basic Diagnostic Coordination, and Patient Administration.

Section 13.2 • Patient Safety Architecture

7 Mandatory Patient Safety Systems in Every Zohran Facility

Safety 01 & 02

Patient Identification & Medication Safety

Two-identifier patient verification at every encounter, allergy alerts in the EMR, medication reconciliation, and high-alert medication controls.

EMR Safeguards
Safety 03 & 04

Infection Control & Diagnostic Follow-Up

Hand hygiene, instrument sterilization, sharps/biohazard waste disposal, and closed-loop tracking of abnormal laboratory and imaging results.

Facility Standards

Emergency Response, Incident Learning & Consent
