Who We Are • United States & Canada

Our Vision, Mission & Core Patient Promises

Zohran Health Group (ZHG) unites 38 specialized medical, surgical, diagnostic, and rehabilitation departments under one patient-first standard of excellence—serving individuals and families across the United States and Canada.

Vision Statement

Our Vision — Trusted Multi-Specialty Healthcare Network

To become the most trusted, modern, and compassionate multi-specialty healthcare network across the United States and Canada—delivering accessible outpatient, specialist, diagnostic, surgical, rehabilitative, and hospital services built on clinical excellence, ethical practice, and continuous innovation.

US & Canada Care Network 38 Medical & Surgical Specialties
Mission Statement

Our Mission — High-Quality, Safe & Patient-Centred Care

To elevate community health across North America by uniting board-certified physicians, surgeons, and compassionate care teams across all 38 specialties—supported by modern medical technology, uncompromising patient safety standards, and seamless in-person and telehealth access.
