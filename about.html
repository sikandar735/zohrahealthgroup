About Zohran Health Group

A premier multi-specialty healthcare and hospital network delivering compassionate, physician-led medical excellence across the United States and Canada.

Who We Are • United States & Canada

Uniting World-Class Doctors Across Every Medical Specialty

Founded by Dr. Sarah, Senior Cardiologist & Heart Surgeon, Zohran Health Group was created with a clear promise: every patient and family across the United States and Canada deserves timely access to compassionate, coordinated, multi-specialty healthcare.

Rather than focusing on only one medical field, Zohran Health Group brings together a wide variety of board-certified physicians, specialist surgeons, nurses, physiotherapists, and diagnostic experts across 38 clinical departments—from Family & Internal Medicine and Cardiology to Neurology, Orthopedics, Pediatrics, Women's Health, Dermatology, Endocrinology, and Surgery.

Our Vision

To become a trusted, innovative, and compassionate healthcare network across the United States and Canada—delivering accessible, high-quality, and patient-centred care through modern medical centres and specialist hospitals.

Our Mission

To deliver safe, ethical, evidence-based, and affordable healthcare through qualified medical professionals, modern clinical infrastructure, digital health systems, and a culture of empathy and accountability.

Dr. Sarah — Founder, Cardiologist & Heart Surgeon

Leading a Comprehensive Network of Doctors & Specialists Across 38 Medical Fields

As an accomplished Cardiologist and Heart Surgeon, Dr. Sarah founded Zohran Health Group to bridge the gap between specialized hospital medicine and accessible everyday family care. Recognizing that patients need more than a single specialty, she established Zohran Health Group as a collaborative home for top physicians and surgeons across Primary Care, Internal Medicine, Cardiology, Neurology, Orthopedics, Pediatrics, Women's Health, Dermatology, Endocrinology, and Diagnostics—working alongside Chairman Mr. Danish Mushtaq and our Board of Directors.

Our Guiding Philosophy • 8 Core Values

What Every Patient Can Expect at Zohran Health Group

Every physician, surgeon, nurse, and team member across our network upholds eight foundational commitments.

Book Your Visit
1. Patient-Centred Care

Your health, dignity, comfort, and personal medical goals come first in every consultation.

2. Clinical Excellence & Safety

Evidence-based medicine, board-certified specialists, and rigorous infection and safety standards.

3. Integrity & Transparency

Honest medical guidance, clear treatment plans, and ethical communication with patients and families.

4. Compassion & Respect

Culturally sensitive, empathetic care that treats every individual like family.

5. Timely Accessibility

Same-week appointments, convenient clinic locations, and barrier-free access across the US and Canada.

6. Modern Medical Innovation

Connected electronic health records, advanced diagnostics, and seamless digital patient services.

7. Multi-Specialty Teamwork

Primary care physicians, specialists, surgeons, and therapists collaborating on your care plan.

8. Community Commitment

Preventive health education, chronic disease support, and lasting wellness for North American communities.
