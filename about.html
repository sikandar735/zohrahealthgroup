About Zohran Health Group

World-class doctors, acclaimed surgeons, and compassionate multi-specialty medical care serving patients and families across the United States and Canada.

United States & Canada Healthcare Network

Excellence Across Every Field of Medicine & Surgery

Zohran Health Group is a premier multi-specialty healthcare organization dedicated to delivering accessible, compassionate, and advanced medical care across the United States and Canada.

Rather than focusing on a single medical discipline, Zohran Health Group unites a diverse roster of board-certified physicians, specialist consultants, and highly skilled surgeons. Founded by renowned Cardiologist & Heart Surgeon Dr. Sarah, our medical centres and specialty clinics provide comprehensive care spanning Cardiology & Cardiothoracic Surgery, Neurology, Orthopedics, Pediatrics, Women's Health, Oncology, Internal & Family Medicine, Dermatology, Endocrinology, Diagnostics, and Rehabilitation.

Diverse Doctors & Surgical Specialists

From heart surgery, neurology, and orthopedics to family medicine, pediatrics, and preventive diagnostics—our specialists collaborate under one unified network so every patient receives complete, personalized care.

Seamless Care Across the US & Canada

We combine state-of-the-art diagnostic imaging, modern surgical and outpatient facilities, digital health records, and same-week specialist appointments to put patients first.
