About Zohran Health Group

World-class multi-specialty healthcare built on clinical excellence, compassionate patient-first service, and seamless care across the United States and Canada.

OUR VISION

Redefining Modern Multi-Specialty Healthcare Across North America

"To become the most trusted, integrated, and patient-centered health system across the United States and Canada—delivering world-class clinical outcomes, compassionate care, and seamless digital health access across every medical and surgical specialty."

OUR MISSION

Six Pillars of Patient-Centered Care

  • Comprehensive Multi-Specialty Excellence: Uniting top physicians and surgeons across 38 departments—from cardiology, oncology, and neurology to orthopedics, pediatrics, and women's health.
  • Evidence-Based Precision Medicine: Combining state-of-the-art diagnostics, robotic surgery, and multidisciplinary care boards for accurate, personalized treatment plans.
  • Uncompromising Patient Safety: Maintaining the highest North American clinical accreditation, infection control, and surgical safety standards.
  • Seamless Cross-Border Accessibility: Coordinating in-person hospital care, outpatient clinics, and virtual telehealth consultations across major US and Canadian cities.
  • Whole-Person Wellness & Prevention: Empowering individuals and families with preventive screenings, rehabilitation, mental health support, and chronic care management.
  • Compassion, Dignity & Transparency: Treating every patient and family with warmth, cultural respect, clear communication, and transparent care navigation.

Founder — Zohran Health Group

Dr. Sarah, MD, FACC, FRCSC

As Founder of Zohran Health Group and a board-certified Senior Cardiologist and Cardiothoracic Heart Surgeon, Dr. Sarah established our institution with a clear vision: to unite North America's finest specialists across all 38 medical and surgical fields under one compassionate, patient-first standard of excellence. While renowned for complex heart surgery and cardiovascular medicine, Dr. Sarah leads an integrated multi-specialty network where oncology, neurology, orthopedics, pediatrics, women's health, and internal medicine thrive together.
