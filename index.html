Recommended Initial Services (Stage A)

Initial Medical Centre Services & Care Pathways

For the initial outpatient medical centre, Zohran Health Group prioritizes 9 high-demand, moderate-capital clinical and administrative services that can be delivered safely and licensed efficiently. View our core services below and click Show More Services to inspect all 9 Stage A service lines.

01 • Family Medicine

Family Medicine & Preventive Primary Care

General consultations, preventive health assessments, routine health screening and structured specialist referrals serving individuals and families.

  • General outpatient medical consultations
  • Preventive health assessments & risk profiling
  • Routine screening & early disease detection
  • Coordinated clinical referrals & follow-up care
Book Consultation Service Scope

02 • Internal Medicine

Internal Medicine & Adult Chronic Care

Comprehensive outpatient assessment and ongoing clinical management of common and complex adult medical conditions.

  • Outpatient diagnostic assessment of adult illnesses
  • Ongoing management of hypertension & cardiometabolic health
  • Multi-system chronic disease monitoring plans
  • Evidence-based medication review & care continuity
Book Consultation Service Scope

03 • Specialist Care

Selected Specialist Consultations

Visiting or scheduled specialist clinics in clinical disciplines supported by local demographic demand and verified physician availability.

  • Scheduled visiting specialist consultation sessions
  • Calibrated to local community epidemiology & demand
  • Integrated EMR clinical documentation & shared care
  • Foundation for Stage B multi-specialty expansion
Book Consultation Service Scope

04 • Rehabilitation

Physiotherapy & Mobility Rehabilitation

Appropriate physical rehabilitation, pain management and mobility restoration services delivered by licensed physiotherapy professionals.

  • Functional mobility & musculoskeletal assessment
  • Post-injury and post-operative rehabilitation programs
  • Dedicated therapy & exercise area within the centre
  • Recurring therapy plans supporting long-term recovery
Book Rehabilitation Service Scope

05 • Dermatology

Dermatology Consultations

Outpatient clinical assessment and management of common skin, hair and nail conditions where specialist availability supports the service.

  • Clinical skin, hair & nail diagnostic evaluation
  • Management of eczema, psoriasis, acne & dermatitis
  • Preventive skin screening & lesion surveillance
  • Outpatient Stage A/B clinical activation pathway
Book Dermatology Service Scope

06 • Endocrinology

Diabetes & Endocrinology Consultations

Structured diabetes monitoring, comprehensive metabolic health assessments, glycemic control pathways and endocrine specialist consultations.

  • Structured Type 1 & Type 2 diabetes care pathways
  • Comprehensive metabolic & thyroid disorder evaluation
  • Continuous glycemic monitoring & complication screening
  • Integrated clinical nutrition & lifestyle coordination
Book Endocrinology Service Scope

07 • Clinical Nutrition

Dietary & Nutrition Services

Evidence-based nutritional assessment, therapeutic meal planning and dietary guidance delivered within the licensed scope of the provider.

  • Individualized clinical nutrition & dietary assessments
  • Therapeutic meal planning for cardiometabolic conditions
  • Weight management & preventive wellness counseling
  • Coordinated care with Family Medicine & Endocrinology
Book Nutrition Service Scope

08 • Diagnostics

Basic Diagnostic Coordination (Lab & Imaging)

On-site specimen collection, laboratory testing through approved third-party or in-house LIS arrangements, and coordinated referrals for diagnostic radiology and imaging.

  • Dedicated on-site blood & specimen collection point
  • Approved LIS laboratory partner testing & fast turnaround
  • Coordinated referrals for ultrasound, X-ray & imaging
  • Direct physician review & patient result follow-up
Book Diagnostics Service Scope

09 • Patient Operations

Administration & Patient Services

Streamlined patient registration, appointment scheduling, secure electronic medical records (EMR/EHR), transparent billing, insurance/third-party payer administration, and structured patient feedback management.

  • Streamlined registration & multi-channel scheduling
  • Secure EMR/EHR digital clinical documentation
  • Transparent billing & third-party payer coordination
  • HIPAA / PIPEDA aligned patient privacy & feedback loops

38 Medical, Surgical & Diagnostic Specialties Across 5 Growth Stages

Zohran Health Group's long-term clinical blueprint encompasses 38 medical, surgical, diagnostic and critical-care departments. Activation of each department is strictly governed by regulatory licensing, facility readiness, verified specialist staffing, and local demand.

  • Initial Stage (Stage A) Family Medicine, Internal Medicine, Physiotherapy, Dermatology, Diabetes & Endocrinology, Dietary Clinic
  • Phased Diagnostic & Specialist Expansion (Stages B & C) Laboratory, Radiology, Cardiology, Neurology, Pediatrics, Orthopaedics, ENT, Ophthalmology, Gastroenterology, HBOT & more
  • Hospital & Advanced Surgical Stage (Stages D & E) Emergency Department (ER), ICU/Critical Care, Anaesthesia, General Surgery, Neurosurgery, Vascular Surgery & Neonatology
