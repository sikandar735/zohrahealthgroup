01

What medical specialties and doctors are available at Zohran Health Group?

Zohran Health Group is a comprehensive multi-specialty healthcare network—we do not limit our care to a single field. Founded by renowned Cardiologist & Heart Surgeon Dr. Sarah, our medical centres unite board-certified doctors and surgeons across Cardiology & Heart Surgery, Neurology & Neurosurgery, Orthopedics & Spine, Pediatrics, Women's Health (OB/GYN), Internal Medicine, Family Medicine, Dermatology, Endocrinology & Diabetes, Oncology, Gastroenterology, Physiotherapy, and Advanced Diagnostics.

02

How can I book an appointment in the United States or Canada?

You can book an appointment online 24/7 through our Appointment Booking page. Simply select your medical specialty, preferred date, and whether you prefer an in-clinic visit or virtual telehealth consultation in the United States or Canada. Our Patient Care Concierge will confirm your appointment promptly.

03

Do I need a referral to see a specialist or surgeon at Zohran Health Group?

Many of our specialist consultations, second surgical opinions, and preventive health checkups can be booked directly without a long wait. If your insurance plan or provincial health system requires a primary care referral, our in-house Family Medicine and Internal Medicine doctors can evaluate you first and coordinate your specialist referral seamlessly.

04

Do you offer virtual telehealth consultations as well as in-person visits?

Yes! Patients across both the United States and Canada can schedule secure video telehealth consultations for primary care, specialist follow-ups, lab and imaging reviews, second opinions, and prescription renewals—as well as full in-person visits at our medical centres.

05

Can I get bloodwork, cardiac testing, and diagnostic imaging done through Zohran Health Group?

Absolutely. Our diagnostic services include full blood and pathology panels, ECG and echocardiography, cardiac stress testing, ultrasound, digital X-ray, and MRI/CT imaging coordination—all linked directly to your doctor so your results are reviewed quickly and clearly.

06

How is my medical privacy protected in the US and Canada?

Your health information is protected by industry-leading encryption and strict compliance with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) in the United States and the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and provincial health privacy statutes in Canada.
