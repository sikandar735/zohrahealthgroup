01 Business Model

Why does Zohran Health Group begin with an Outpatient Medical Centre (Stage A) before building a full hospital?

In both the United States and Canada, acute-care hospitals require substantial capital expenditure, complex multi-year facility licensing, accreditation, and large clinical workforces. Launching with a Stage A Outpatient Medical Centre allows Zohran Health Group to begin operations with manageable capital, establish immediate community trust and referral networks, generate early operational cash flow, and expand into Stages B, C, D (Hospital), and E based on verified demand.

02 Initial Scope

Which services are offered in the initial Stage A Medical Centre?

Zohran Health Group Clinical Network specifies 9 recommended initial services: (1) Family Medicine, (2) Internal Medicine, (3) Selected Specialist Consultations (visiting or part-time), (4) Physiotherapy, (5) Dermatology, (6) Diabetes & Endocrinology Consultations, (7) Dietary & Nutrition Services, (8) Basic Diagnostic Coordination (sample collection, ECG, point-of-care testing, and accredited lab/imaging referrals), and (9) Administration & Patient Services.

03 Long-Term Plan

How are the 38 planned departments organized across the expansion stages?

Zohran Health Group Clinical Network categorizes all 38 specialties by their target activation stage: Initial Stage specialties (Family Medicine, Internal Medicine, Physiotherapy, Dermatology, Diabetes & Endocrinology, Dietary Clinic), Diagnostic Core (Laboratory, Radiology & Imaging, Chemical Pathology), Specialist Expansion (Cardiology, Neurology, Orthopaedics, Paediatrics, OB/GYN, ENT, Ophthalmology, Psychiatry, Pulmonology, Rheumatology, Urology, etc.), and Hospital/Surgical Stage (Emergency Department/ER, ICU/Critical Care, Anaesthesia, General Surgery, Neurosurgery, Vascular Surgery, Neonatology, Oncology).

04 Governance

How is Zohran Health Group governed and what is the verification policy for leadership?

Zohran Health Group is led by Founder Dr. Sarah and Chairman Mr. Danish Mushtaq, governed by the Board of Directors (Sakandar Hayat, Dr. Laraib Usman, Muhammad Yahya, and Ms. Emily), and supported by Health Director Dr. Awias Ur Rehman, Project Lead Neil Robert, and Team Management (HR, HR Executive, and clinical/corporate operations), alongside 5 specialized Board Committees (Finance, Audit & Risk; Clinical Quality & Patient Safety; Governance, Ethics & Compliance; HR & Remuneration; Strategy, Investment & Development). Per Zohran Health Group Clinical Network, verified biographies, academic credentials, professional licenses, and written consents are formally attached prior to regulatory submission or investor due diligence.

05 Investment & Partnerships

What types of strategic partners and investors can collaborate with Zohran Health Group?

Zohran Health Group Clinical Network invites collaboration across 8 partner categories: (1) Equity Investors, (2) Healthcare Operators, (3) Physicians & Specialist Groups, (4) Diagnostic Laboratory & Imaging Providers, (5) Medical Equipment Suppliers, (6) Healthcare Technology/EMR Providers, (7) Property & Real Estate Development Partners, and (8) Financial, Legal & Regulatory Advisors.

06 Facility & Privacy

How are facility design and patient data privacy managed in the USA and Canada?

The initial facility is organized into 8 functional clinical and administrative zones meeting infection control, ADA/accessibility, fire safety, and HVAC standards. Digital operations run on 9 integrated systems engineered for full compliance with HIPAA in the United States and PIPEDA plus provincial health information privacy laws in Canada.
