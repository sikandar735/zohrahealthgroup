01

Institutional Purpose & Proposal Scope

This platform presents the official Investment, Partnership & Healthcare Development Proposal of Zohran Health Group for the establishment of an initial outpatient medical centre (Stage A) and phased expansion into a multi-specialty hospital network (Stages B–E) in the United States and Canada.

All clinical services, diagnostic capabilities, and inpatient hospital departments described herein are subject to applicable federal, state, provincial, and municipal healthcare licensing prior to operational launch.

02

Section 6 Mandatory Leadership & Credential Verification Protocol

Section 6 of the Zohran Health Group Proposal identifies Founder Dr. Sarah, Chairman Mr. Danish Mushtaq, Board of Directors (Sakandar Hayat, Dr. Laraib Usman, Muhammad Yahya, and Ms. Emily), Health Director Dr. Awias Ur Rehman, Project Lead Neil Robert, and Team Management (HR, HR Executive, and target clinical/corporate management roles in Section 8).

Mandatory Verification Requirement

Before regulatory submission, investor due diligence, or public clinical release, Zohran Health Group requires verified biographies, primary-source academic qualifications, active state/provincial professional registrations/licenses, and written consent of all named individuals.

03

United States & Canada Regulatory Compliance

Zohran Health Group is committed to full compliance with all healthcare statutes in its target jurisdictions:

US state medical clinic & facility licensing
Canadian provincial college & ministry rules
Corporate practice of medicine (CPOM) compliance
HIPAA (USA) & PIPEDA/provincial privacy laws
04

Clinical Governance & Patient Safety Mandate

Every clinical encounter is governed by written standard operating procedures, licensed physician/nurse credentialing, two-identifier patient verification, medication safety checks, infection prevention, and documented informed consent.

05

Phased Expansion & Financial Discipline

Progression from Stage A (Initial Outpatient Medical Centre) to Stage B (Multi-Specialty), Stage C (Procedural/Diagnostic), Stage D (Full Hospital), and Stage E (Network) requires formal approval from the Board of Directors and Strategy, Investment & Development Committee based on verified KPIs.
