01

Multi-Specialty Healthcare Services

Zohran Health Group provides comprehensive medical, surgical, diagnostic, and rehabilitation services across the United States and Canada—including Cardiology & Heart Surgery, Neurology, Orthopedics, Pediatrics, Women's Health, Internal Medicine, Family Medicine, and specialty clinics.

Information on this website is provided to help patients learn about our doctors, departments, and services and to schedule consultations with licensed healthcare professionals.

02

Appointment Scheduling & Virtual Telehealth

Patients may request in-person clinic visits, specialist consultations, diagnostic screenings, or virtual video telehealth sessions through our online booking portal or Patient Care Concierge. For life-threatening medical emergencies, always call 911 or visit the nearest emergency department immediately.

03

United States & Canada Healthcare Compliance

Zohran Health Group upholds rigorous medical standards across all operating jurisdictions:

Board-certified US & Canadian physicians and surgeons
Accredited clinical laboratory & diagnostic imaging standards
Multidisciplinary surgical & clinical safety protocols
Full US HIPAA & Canadian PIPEDA patient data protection
04

Patient Rights, Safety & Informed Consent

Every patient at Zohran Health Group is treated with dignity, respect, and transparency. All diagnostic procedures, surgical treatments, and specialist care plans are explained clearly so you can make fully informed decisions about your health.
