Section 11.2 • Technical & Engineering Standards

7 Mandatory Facility & Building Code Requirements

1. Healthcare Zoning & Occupancy Approval: Municipal healthcare clinic zoning and certificate of occupancy in the selected US state or Canadian province.

2. Universal Accessibility (ADA / Provincial Codes): Barrier-free entrances, ramps/elevators, wide corridors, and accessible washrooms for mobility-impaired patients.

3. Infection-Control Finishes: Washable, non-porous clinical flooring, antimicrobial worktops, and hand-washing sinks in every clinical room.

4. HVAC & Clinical Ventilation: Appropriate air exchange rates, temperature/humidity control, and filtration for clinical and specimen areas.

5. Fire Safety & Emergency Egress: Illuminated exit routes, fire detection/suppression systems, and emergency backup lighting.

6. Acoustic Privacy & Secure IT Cabling: Sound-attenuated consultation walls (HIPAA/PIPEDA speech privacy) and redundant network connectivity.

7. Future Expansion Readiness: Structural and electrical capacity or adjacent suite options to accommodate Stage B/C imaging and specialty expansion.
