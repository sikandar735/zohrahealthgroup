Your Patient Rights
01

US HIPAA & Canadian PIPEDA Compliance

In the United States, all clinical and administrative workflows comply strictly with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) Privacy and Security Rules, as well as state health privacy laws.

In Canada, all patient services comply with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and provincial health information statutes (including PHIPA, HIA, and PIPA).

02

How We Use & Protect Your Health Information

Zohran Health Group collects and processes patient information solely to deliver safe, coordinated medical care:

Clinical & Medical Records

  • Patient registration, contact details & appointment requests
  • Doctor consultation notes, medical history & prescriptions
  • Laboratory bloodwork, cardiac testing & imaging reports
  • Insurance coordination & specialist referral records

Telehealth & Digital Portal

  • Encrypted video telehealth consultations
  • Secure patient portal messaging with your care team
  • Digital appointment reminders and follow-up instructions
  • Strict role-based access limited to your treating care team
03

Advanced Healthcare Cybersecurity

We safeguard every patient record with enterprise-grade technical and organizational security controls:

Role-Based Access Control & Multi-Factor Authentication
End-to-End Encryption in Transit (TLS) & at Rest
Continuous Security Monitoring & Protected Health Records
Zero Sale or Unauthorized Sharing of Patient Data
04

Your Patient Privacy Rights

You have the right to access your medical records, request updates or corrections, receive clear information about how your health data is used, and provide informed consent prior to any consultation, diagnostic test, or surgical procedure.
